Identity

Where Vision
Meets Structure.

Bill Jobs is not about appearing bigger. It is about appearing precise, credible and culturally aware.

Creative Producer and Director with over 20 years of experience managing end-to-end productions across music video, fashion, beauty, branded content, corporate communication and live events.

He has worked for major Italian labels — Sony Music, Universal, EMI — fashion brands including Elisabetta Franchi and Anteprima, luxury beauty clients Cotril, Wella and Gamma+, and corporate institutions BMW, PwC, UniCredit and Confindustria.

Production is not merely logistics. It is emotional architecture. Ideas need structure to become real.

Massimiliano Di Liberto
Founder · Bill Jobs Production · Salò, Italy
Expertise

Full-Spectrum Creative Production

01

Creative Direction & Direction

Film & video direction, visual concept development, cinematic storytelling, art direction, set design. Music video, commercial, fashion, corporate, event.

02

Production Management

End-to-end production: pre-production to delivery. Budget & scheduling, crew coordination (up to 60 people), location scouting, executive production.

03

Post-Production

Video editing (narrative, rhythmic, music-led), color grading (cinematic look), VFX & compositing, motion graphics & 3D, broadcast & digital finalisation.

04

Events & Live Production

Fairs, live shows, institutional events. Complete direction from concept to live execution — video, audio, scenography, multi-screen content.

05

Branded Content & Fashion

Fashion films, beauty campaigns, runway visuals, branded storytelling. Clients: Elisabetta Franchi, Anteprima, Cotril, Wella, Gamma+.

06

AI-Assisted Workflows

Generative AI for content, concept development and production optimisation. Tools: DaVinci Resolve, Blender, Cinema 4D, Unreal Engine, After Effects.
